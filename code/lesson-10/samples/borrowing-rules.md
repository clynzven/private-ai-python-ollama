# Borrowing rules

Anyone who lives, works or studies in Maple Grove can get a free library card.
Bring a photo ID and proof of address to the front desk.

## Loans
- Books and audiobooks: 3 weeks
- DVDs and board games: 1 week
- You can borrow up to 12 items at the same time.
- You can renew an item twice, online or at the desk, unless someone has reserved it.

## Late returns
Children and teenagers never pay late fees.
Adults pay 20 cents per item per day, up to a maximum of 5 euros per item.

## Lost or damaged items
Tell us as soon as possible. You pay the replacement price of the item.
