Wi-Fi and printing

Free Wi-Fi is available everywhere in the library. The network is called MapleGuest.
The password changes on the first day of every month; ask at the front desk or look at the
screen next to the entrance.

Printing and copying:
black and white costs 10 cents per page, colour costs 50 cents per page.
You can print from the library computers or send a file to print@maplegrove-library.example
and pick it up at the desk within 3 days.

The library computers log you out after 2 hours. You can book a computer for longer
at the front desk.
