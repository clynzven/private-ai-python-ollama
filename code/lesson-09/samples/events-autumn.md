# Autumn events

## Python for Beginners club
Every Thursday from 18:00 to 19:30 in the Makerspace. Bring your own laptop.
No experience needed. Free, but please sign up at the front desk.

## Story time for toddlers
Every Tuesday and Friday at 10:30 in the children's corner. Free, no sign-up needed.

## Repair café
The first Saturday of every month from 11:00 to 15:00. Volunteers help you repair
clothes, bikes, lamps and small electronics.

## Author talk
On 14 November at 19:00, local author Rosa Linde talks about her new crime novel
"The Silent Harbour". Tickets cost 5 euros.
